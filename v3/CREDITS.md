# CREDITS · v3

## Premium concept car prototype

The v3 premium vehicle adapter loads the **Car Concept** glTF asset from the Khronos Group glTF Sample Assets repository at runtime.

- Asset: Car Concept
- Model and textures: Eric Chadwick / Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- Upstream: KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets · `Models/CarConcept`
- Original upstream asset also contains Khronos / 3D Commerce logo material; the v3 adapter attempts to hide meshes/materials whose names identify those logo elements.

The model is used as a **visual prototype for the 2027 concept supercar**. It is not presented as an official Ferrari 2027 vehicle or as a production vehicle design.
